ABOUT ME

My name is Leevi Kohtala. I am a 20 year old student at Södertörns Högskola, studying journalism. I have a background in basketball going back to my childhood. On this page you will get a rundown of my passion for the sport and why I made this website.

MY OWN PLAYING EXPIRIENCE

I started playing basketball at the age of 10 in a club called "Viby Basket" in Sollentuna, Sweden. I was a tall kid, reaching the height of 6 foot 3 at the age of 13. This meant that for the majority of my playing career, I played as a wing/center. I spent most of my time playing in division 2, being the second star on a team that regularly finished as the top team in our division.

MY TINY COACHING EXPIRIENCE

At the age of 15 I decided to coach a youth team of 13 year olds for one season. During my time as a coach I got tasked with developing the second string players that had less playing expirience compared to the divison 1 team. I led the second string players to a 10-6 record, which in all honesty was probably more due to the fast development of the players, rather than some incredible coaching effort for me.

MY NBA FANDOM

I started watching NBA at the bright age of 10, and have been heavily invested in the league since I turned 12. This includes me watching a lot of games and analyzing the sport of basketball that gets played at the highest level. I am personally a Lakers fan, which probably shows in my clear bias in the "Top 5 standout players" list.
