MY TOP 5 STANDOUT PLAYERS

These five NBA players have caught my attention this season the most for different reasons. Keep in mind that this is not a list of the best players in the league, but the most surprising.

1. LUKA DONČIC

Los Angeles Lakers | PG/SG

39.0 PPG

7.2 RPG

7.8 AST

"He is the MVP" - Leevi's Take

Where can I even start? This season Luka has been on another level. He is showing the league why he is the best player in the world and on another level from his peers like Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and Victor Wembanyama. Eventough Luka has been amazing for many years, this season he has entered his true prime. In the great words of Stephen A. Smith "HE'S AVERAGING FORTY!"

2. STEPH CURRY

Golden State Warriors | PG

27.8 PPG

3.7 RPG

3.9 AST

"Unc still got it" - Leevi's Take

For multiple seasons in a row. I have had the feeling that Steph Curry has been a bit uninterested on the basketball court at times due to how bad the Warriors have been. This offseason they acquired the 24-year old rookie in Yaxel Lendebourgh, who has flipped the team around completely. As the Warriors look competent for the first time in years. Steph Curry has clearly entered the season with a pep in his step.

3. DAVION MITCHELL

Miami Heat | PG

16.7 PPG

2.4 RPG

9.2 AST

"Welcome back Gary Payton" - Leevi's Take

Davion Mitchell is the most surprising role player ascension of the season so far. He has been the perfect complimentary piece around Giannis and Bam, with his efficient three-point shooting. He is also leading the league in assists per game while keeping a very low turnover ratio. When Giannis got traded to the Heat. people said that the team needs a better point guard. Davion Mitchell this season has silenced the critics.

4. MATISSE THYBULLE

Los Angeles Lakers | SF/SG

7.4 PPG

2.2 STLS

0.9 BLKS

"Kawhi Leonard if The Monstars stole his offensive powers" - Leevi's Take

Matisse Thybulle this season has been the anchor to the Lakers perimeter defense, after the team lost Marcus Smart in the offseason. Thybulle has been a lights out catch-and-shooter, hitting around 39% of his three-point shots. He is also second in steals in the entire league.

5. MO BAMBA

Utah Jazz | C

9.3 PPG

5.8 RPG

1.8 BLKS

"What Happened!?" - Leevi's Take

Mohamed Bamba was the 6th pick in the 2018 draft. After trying to find his footing in the league during his first 4 years, he struggled heavily at the things that make big men effective. He eventually fell out of the league and struggled to find his way back for a few years, but that has changed this season. Mo Bamba is now a completely different player. He is locked in on defense and doesn't chuck up three-pointers the way that he did in his earlier years. The big man has finally realized that he is a man who is big.
